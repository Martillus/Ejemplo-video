Inside the studio

One room, nine nodes, and a very cold floor.

We model, shade and light objects — bottles, lamps, tools, type. No characters, no crowds. Everything is sculpted in the room at 112 Render Lane and pushed to the farm overnight, so the first pass is sitting in your inbox before the second coffee.

Visits are welcome Tuesday through Sunday. Bring the physical object if there is one; we will scan it while you wait.

How a brief moves

Three stops between sketch and delivery.

  1. 01

    Brief & boards

    You send references, dimensions and the one shot that has to work. We come back with a board and a frame count the same week.

  2. 02

    Sculpt & look-dev

    Mesh, then materials. You see a lit still before a single frame goes to the farm, so the surface is signed off while it is still cheap to change.

  3. 03

    Farm & delivery

    All-intra masters at 1920×1080, plus stills, alphas and the turntable loop. Delivered by seven, cut to whatever the viewport needs.

From a screen recording to a worker on shift

  1. Step 01

    Record the process once

    Someone who already does the job walks through it in the browser. Axon turns that session into a draft procedure, in steps you can read.

  2. Step 02

    Supervise the first runs

    The worker asks before it commits anything. You approve or correct, and every correction is folded back into the procedure.

  3. Step 03

    Put it on shift

    Run it on a schedule, on a queue or on a webhook. Exceptions route to a human with the full session replay attached.
